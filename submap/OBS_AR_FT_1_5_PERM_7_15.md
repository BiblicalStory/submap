**Title:** (AIS) The Argument of Mark, S. Baylis

The gospel of Mark presents Jesus as the Isaianic Servant, whose authority is demonstrated through His teachings, healings, and the establishment of a new community of disciples empowered by the Spirit. The narrative unfolds through a series of conflicts with Jewish leaders, culminating in Jesus' death and resurrection, which secures salvation and commissions the disciples to spread the gospel.
