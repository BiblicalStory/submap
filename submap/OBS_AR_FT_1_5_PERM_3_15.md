**Title:** Argument Summary of John

The argument presented in the Gospel of John portrays Jesus as the fulfillment of Old Testament themes, particularly in relation to the crises introduced in Genesis. Through various narratives, John illustrates how Israel, represented as the greater Cain, rejects Jesus, the greater Abel, while simultaneously fulfilling prophetic scripture. The text emphasizes the transition from the old covenant to the new, highlighting Jesus' role as the true Temple and the embodiment of life-giving sacrifice. The narrative culminates in the resurrection, which not only affirms Jesus' identity but also establishes the foundation for the disciples' mission in the world.
