**Title:** **The Literary Argument of the Book of Psalms (AIS)**

The Book of Psalms presents a theological narrative that emphasizes God's covenant faithfulness, particularly through the figure of the Davidic King, who is portrayed as a pivotal character in God's plan for redemption and restoration. The Psalter explores themes of lovingkindness, righteousness, and the significance of Zion as a symbol of God's enduring kingdom. It articulates the tension between human sinfulness and divine promise, ultimately pointing to a future hope in the coming Seed of David. The structure of the Psalms is carefully crafted to reflect these themes, guiding the reader through Israel's historical crises and God's sovereign response.
